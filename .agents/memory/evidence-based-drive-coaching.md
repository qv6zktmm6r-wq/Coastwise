---
name: Evidence-based drive coaching
description: Safety boundary for spoken prompts based on the sensors and context Coastwise actually has.
---

Drive coaching must distinguish general observation prompts from factual claims. Ask the learner to scan for signs, traffic, people, hazards, and changing conditions, but never say those things are present unless Coastwise has direct evidence. Speed guidance may state a trustworthy GPS estimate and ask the learner to compare it with the posted limit; it must not claim speeding without verified speed-limit data.

**Why:** Unsupported statements make the coach feel unreliable and can distract a learner during a real drive.

**How to apply:** Review every new spoken cue against its data source. If the app lacks computer vision, mapped speed limits, traffic detection, or another direct signal, phrase the cue as a scan or self-check rather than an observation.
