---
name: Coastwise family sharing
description: Product boundary for optional cross-device family sync and device-local drive video.
---

Keep Coastwise installable and usable without an account. An account is optional and enables permission-based, conflict-safe family progress sync. Drive recordings, precise routes, route titles, coaching positions, annotations, and free-form drive notes remain owned by the device.

**Why:** Families need cross-device progress without making accounts mandatory or silently uploading sensitive teen driving video or precise location history.

**How to apply:** Sync only practice data, goals, settings, and privacy-minimized drive-log facts. Preserve stable drive IDs, local use, backups, resets, revocable roles, explicit conflicts, and device-local reviews/notes/route titles. Require explicit consent before broadening sync.
