---
name: Coastwise family sharing
description: Product boundary between local family summaries and future cross-device account sync.
---

Keep Coastwise local-first features explicit: family progress and drive summaries can be shared from the device, but they are not live multi-device sync.

**Why:** Parent/student accounts require authenticated, permission-based storage and conflict handling. Video is especially sensitive and must not upload implicitly.

**How to apply:** When adding cloud family plans, preserve local data migration, revocable role permissions, and device-local video by default. Require explicit consent before any recording upload.
