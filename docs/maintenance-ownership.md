# Content maintenance ownership

The content release owner maintains the six-state source inventory and
coordinates the accountable human review for each jurisdiction. The owner
checks the official handbook, licensing/GDL pages, testing/document pages, and
statutes at least monthly and immediately after an agency publishes a new
edition, amendment, emergency rule, or correction.

## Review process

1. Record the exact official URL, source title, revision/effective metadata
   (or explicitly record that the source does not publish one), and check date
   in the jurisdiction source matrix.
2. Compare numerical, age-dependent, permit, supervision, practice-hour,
   restriction, testing, and documentation claims against the source.
3. Run the six-state content, manifest, typecheck, privacy, and release gates.
4. Obtain a named accountable human reviewer and dated approval before
   changing a jurisdiction from pending to approved.
5. Publish a correction notice for wording/source metadata changes and a
   material notice with the official source link when a rule or eligibility
   requirement changes.

Escalate immediately to the content release owner and accountable reviewer
when a source is unavailable, contradictory, materially amended, or missing
effective/revision information. Do not infer legal requirements from an
undated page or from another state. Product and engineering owners may
revert a pack, but may not silently waive human legal/editorial approval.
